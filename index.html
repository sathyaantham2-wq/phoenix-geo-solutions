Our Areas of Expertise

Integrated solutions across the mineral value chain

From the first reconnaissance survey to cross-border delivery, we bring scientific rigour and commercial clarity to every stage.

Geological Consultancy & Mineral Exploration

Our core discipline — comprehensive field, laboratory and desk-based investigation to identify and quantify mineral potential.

  • Geological and structural mapping
  • Reconnaissance and detailed mineral exploration
  • Geochemical and geophysical investigations
  • Remote sensing, GIS, DGPS, drone and topographical surveys
  • Exploration drilling, core logging and sampling
  • Resource assessment and mineral deposit evaluation
  • Technical reports, mining plans and statutory documentation*

Industrial Minerals

Exploration, evaluation and commercial development of quartz, feldspar, limestone, dolomite, granite, marble, laterite, graphite and other industrial minerals.

Metallic Minerals

Exploration and assessment of gold, manganese, copper, iron ore, chromite, nickel, cobalt and other metallic resources, guided by geological potential and project requirements.

Rare Earth, Critical & Strategic Minerals

Geological assessment and sourcing for rare earth elements, lithium, niobium, tantalum, zirconium, graphite and other critical commodities, subject to analytical verification and regulatory feasibility.

Beach Sand & Heavy Minerals

Investigations and commercial opportunities relating to ilmenite, rutile, zircon, garnet, sillimanite and monazite-bearing deposits, within applicable regulatory approvals.

Mineral Trading, Imports & Exports

Domestic and international sourcing, procurement, supply and cross-border marketing of eligible mineral commodities, linking mine owners, processors, manufacturers and bulk buyers.

International Projects & Partnerships

Developing partnerships across India, Africa, the Middle East and other mineral-rich regions — exploration projects, technical collaborations and mineral supply chains.

*Subject to applicable qualifications, approvals and regulations.
